# 📊 Customer Purchase Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer Purchase Analysis is a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, product performance, and category-wise sales.

The project provides interactive visualizations and business insights to help understand customer spending patterns and identify high-performing products and categories.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Understand monthly sales trends
- Identify top-selling products
- Identify high-spending customers
- Analyze sales by category
- Provide actionable business recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Quantity
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Top Products by Sales
- Top 10 Customers by Spending
- Interactive filters for Date, Category, Gender and City

## 💡 Key Insights

- Total sales are approximately ₹4M.
- Laptop is the highest-selling product.
- Nikhil is the highest-spending customer.
- Electronics is the best-performing category.
- High-performing products and key customers can be targeted for future growth strategies.
